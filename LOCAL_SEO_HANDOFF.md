## Publishing checklist

1. Claim and verify the Google Business Profile, then set the exact canonical
   name, category, address, main telephone, website, appointment link, hours,
   and map pin above.
2. Add current exterior, interior, practitioner, and treatment-room photos.
   Use descriptive filenames and captions; replace outdated photos rather than
   duplicating them.
3. Claim or update the equivalent profiles in Bing Places and Apple Business
   Connect. Reuse the canonical information exactly.
4. Check the official local business directory and the professional college
   directory for any existing entry. Correct inconsistencies rather than
   submitting duplicate listings.
5. Create a simple listing log with the profile URL, owner, verification
   status, date updated, and next review date. Recheck all listings every
   quarter and whenever contact details or hours change.

## Review workflow

After an appointment, invite patients to leave an honest review using the
Google Business Profile review link. Do not offer incentives, write reviews on
behalf of patients, gate feedback, or request reviews only from satisfied
patients. Reply to reviews promptly without disclosing patient information.

Suggested Spanish message:

> experiencia, puedes dejar una reseña honesta en Google. Tu opinión ayuda a
> otras personas de Urnieta y Gipuzkoa a encontrarnos.
